# Grilling

Explore decisions that could change the plan. Use the conversation and available
evidence first; investigate factual questions yourself.

Ask related independent questions together, with a recommendation and its reason.
Wait for answers before decisions that depend on them. Keep each round manageable;
ask only questions whose answers can materially change the outcome.

Challenge contradictions and examine concrete scenarios. Use the user's answers
to decide what still needs exploration. Do not expand into every imaginable branch
or re-ask a settled choice.

Finish when the meaningful uncertainties are resolved or clearly assigned, and
state the resulting direction. Continue any already-authorized implementation;
a request only for discussion does not authorize building or publishing.
